FIVE SCENARIOS FOR DESCRIPTIVE, DIAGNOSTIC, PREDICTIVE, AND PRESCRIPTIVE ANALYSIS

TASK: Perform Four Types of Analytics on Five Real-World Scenarios

============================================================
SCENARIO 1: EMPLOYEE ATTENDANCE AND PRODUCTIVITY ANALYSIS

Problem:
Employees have frequent absences, late arrivals, or declining productivity.

1. Descriptive Analysis — What happened?
   - Calculate attendance percentage, number of absences, late arrivals, and average working hours.
   - Summarize trends by department and month.

2. Diagnostic Analysis — Why did it happen?
   - Identify departments, weekdays, or periods with higher absenteeism.
   - Compare attendance patterns across teams and time periods.

3. Predictive Analysis — What may happen next?
   - Forecast attendance trends for the next month using historical records.
   - Identify periods when staffing shortages may occur.

4. Prescriptive Analysis — What should be done?
   - Recommend staffing adjustments, attendance alerts, or workload balancing.
   - Suggest areas where further investigation may be useful.

Suggested dataset fields:
Employee ID, department, date, attendance status, late arrival, working hours.

Expected deliverable:
An attendance analysis report with charts, findings, and recommendations.

============================================================
SCENARIO 2: HOSPITAL PATIENT WAITING-TIME ANALYSIS

Problem:
Patients experience long waiting times before consultations.

1. Descriptive Analysis — What happened?
   - Calculate average waiting time, patient volume, and daily admissions.
   - Summarize waiting times by department and time of day.

2. Diagnostic Analysis — Why did it happen?
   - Investigate whether delays are associated with peak hours, staff availability, or department workload.
   - Compare waiting times across departments and days.

3. Predictive Analysis — What may happen next?
   - Forecast patient volume and likely waiting-time trends using historical data.
   - Identify periods that may experience higher demand.

4. Prescriptive Analysis — What should be done?
   - Recommend appointment scheduling changes and staff allocation.
   - Suggest ways to reduce congestion during busy periods.

Suggested dataset fields:
Patient arrival time, consultation start time, department, staff availability, waiting duration.

Expected deliverable:
A patient waiting-time report with trends, possible causes, forecasts, and recommendations.

Note:
Use synthetic or anonymized data. Do not include identifiable patient information.

============================================================
SCENARIO 3: SOFTWARE DEFECT AND BUG ANALYSIS

Problem:
A software application has recurring defects and a high number of failed test cases.

1. Descriptive Analysis — What happened?
   - Calculate total bugs, severity distribution, defect closure rate, and test pass percentage.
   - Summarize defects by software module and release.

2. Diagnostic Analysis — Why did it happen?
   - Identify modules or release stages associated with recurring defects.
   - Review defect descriptions, code changes, and test results to investigate likely causes.

3. Predictive Analysis — What may happen next?
   - Estimate which modules or upcoming releases may have higher defect risk using historical data.
   - Identify patterns associated with repeated failures.

4. Prescriptive Analysis — What should be done?
   - Recommend prioritizing high-risk modules, adding automated tests, and performing code reviews.
   - Suggest regression testing for areas with recurring defects.

Suggested dataset fields:
Bug ID, software module, severity, creation date, closure date, release, test status.

Expected deliverable:
A software quality report with defect trends, risk estimates, and improvement actions.

============================================================
SCENARIO 4: IT SUPPORT TICKET AND SLA ANALYSIS

Problem:
The IT support team receives many tickets and some are not resolved within their Service Level Agreement (SLA) deadlines.

1. Descriptive Analysis — What happened?
   - Measure ticket volume, average resolution time, open tickets, and SLA compliance.
   - Summarize ticket counts by category, priority, and team.

2. Diagnostic Analysis — Why did it happen?
   - Identify ticket categories, teams, or time periods associated with delays.
   - Investigate common causes of repeated incidents and SLA breaches.

3. Predictive Analysis — What may happen next?
   - Estimate future ticket volume and the risk of SLA breaches from historical data.
   - Identify categories likely to require additional support.

4. Prescriptive Analysis — What should be done?
   - Recommend ticket prioritization, automated routing, and staffing adjustments during peak periods.
   - Suggest a knowledge base or self-service guidance for common issues.

Suggested dataset fields:
Ticket ID, category, priority, assigned team, creation time, resolution time, SLA status.

Expected deliverable:
An IT support performance report with SLA metrics, likely causes, forecasts, and recommendations.

============================================================
SCENARIO 5: CI/CD PIPELINE PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS


Problem:
Software builds and deployments are slow or frequently fail.

1. Descriptive Analysis — What happened?
   - Calculate build success rate, average build duration, deployment frequency, and failure count.
   - Summarize failures by pipeline stage and project.

2. Diagnostic Analysis — Why did it happen?
   - Analyze pipeline logs to identify failed tests, dependency errors, configuration issues, or deployment failures.
   - Compare build durations and failures across stages.

3. Predictive Analysis — What may happen next?
   - Estimate build failure risk or expected pipeline duration using previous runs.
   - Identify patterns that may indicate an increased risk of future failures.

4. Prescriptive Analysis — What should be done?
   - Recommend caching dependencies, parallelizing tests, and improving pipeline validation.
   - Suggest alerts for repeated failures and unusually long builds.

Suggested dataset fields:
Build ID, project, start time, duration, status, failed stage, deployment result.

Expected deliverable:
A CI/CD performance report with build metrics, failure analysis, forecasts, and improvement actions.
